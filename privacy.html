Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 30, 2026

This policy covers the Duskfold iPhone app, published by Derek Zak (“we”). The short version: we have no accounts and no servers, and we do not collect your personal information ourselves. Advertising is provided by Google AdMob, which collects some data described below.

What stays on your device

Your game progress, settings and chosen options are stored on your device only (in the app’s local storage). We never receive them. Deleting the app deletes them.

Purchases

Purchases are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We do not see your payment details or name. Apple provides the app with confirmation of what you have bought so it can unlock the content. Restore Purchases asks Apple for that confirmation.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version can show an optional rewarded video ad that you choose to watch in exchange for a hint. Ads are served by Google AdMob using the Google Mobile Ads SDK. We show no banner or interstitial ads. Players who buy Duskfold Full Game do not see ads.

When an ad is requested or shown, Google may collect:

  • Device identifiers, including the advertising identifier (IDFA) only if you allow tracking in the App Tracking Transparency prompt, and other app- or developer-scoped identifiers
  • Your IP address, used to estimate your approximate (coarse) location
  • Advertising data and ad interactions, such as which ads were shown, video views and taps
  • Performance data and crash and diagnostic data from the ads SDK

Google uses this to deliver and measure ads, and for analytics and fraud prevention. We do not receive this data in a way that identifies you. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services and Google’s Privacy Policy.

Tracking choices

  • App Tracking Transparency: where Apple requires it, the app asks permission before tracking. If you decline, the advertising identifier is not available and ads are not personalized using it. You can change this in iOS Settings, Privacy & Security, Tracking.
  • Consent (EEA, UK, Switzerland): in these regions the app shows Google’s User Messaging Platform consent form before requesting ads. You can change your choice later where the app provides a privacy options entry.
  • Limit ad personalization: you can also turn off personalized ads in iOS Settings, Privacy & Security, Apple Advertising.

What we do not collect

We do not have user accounts and do not collect your name, email, contacts, precise location, photos, or any other personal information. We run no analytics or advertising servers of our own. If you email us for support, we receive your email address and message and use it only to reply.

Children

Duskfold is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your rights

Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access or delete. For data held by Google, use the controls above or Google’s privacy tools. Depending on where you live (for example under GDPR or CCPA) you may have rights regarding that data, which you can exercise with Google or Apple.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date.

Duskfold

Two worlds. One fold.

Duskfold is a puzzle game for iPhone. Every level exists twice, once in the light and once in the dark. Swipe to move one tile, then fold to swap worlds and open a path that was not there a moment ago.

Fold with the on-screen button, a two-finger pinch, or by physically folding an iPhone Duo. It works on every iPhone and is optimized for both Duo screens.

What is inside

  • 100 handcrafted levels in five chapters
  • Crates, levers, gates and cracked floors that behave differently in each world
  • Unlimited undo and restart
  • No timers, no lives, no energy
  • Chapter 1 is free. A one-time purchase unlocks the rest, with no subscription
Coming soon to the App Store. For iPhone, iOS 17 or later.

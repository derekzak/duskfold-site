Support

Need help? Email zak.support12@gmail.com and include your iPhone model and iOS version.

How do I fold?

Tap the Fold button, pinch with two fingers on the board, or on an iPhone Duo, fold the device. Each fold swaps between the light and dark world while your character stays on the same tile.

How do I move?

Swipe in the direction you want to go. You move one tile at a time.

How do I undo or restart?

Use the undo and restart buttons in the game. Both are unlimited and free. Nothing is lost for using them.

What are shards and fold limits?

Shards are collectibles placed on some levels. Some levels also limit how many times you can fold, shown on screen during play.

How do hints work?

If you are stuck, a hint shows the next few moves. On the free version you can watch an optional rewarded video ad to get a hint. Ads never appear any other time. With the full game purchase, hints are unlimited and there are no ads.

What is free and what is paid?

Chapter 1 (levels 1 to 20) is free. The one-time purchase, Duskfold Full Game, unlocks chapters 2 to 5 (80 more levels), unlimited hints, and removes ads. It is not a subscription.

How do I restore my purchase?

Open Settings in the game and tap Restore Purchases. Use the same Apple Account you bought with. If it does not appear, check that you are signed in to the App Store, then email us.

I changed phones. Is my progress saved?

Progress is stored only on your device, not on a server, so it does not move to a new phone automatically. Purchases can be restored on any device with the same Apple Account.

Tips for iPhone Duo

Close the hinge past about 75 degrees to fold once; open it past about 105 degrees to arm the next fold. Resting in a book pose will not trigger a fold. The inner screen plays in landscape with a side panel. If the hinge is not responding, you can always use the Fold button or the pinch gesture.

Does it work on my iPhone?

Duskfold requires iOS 17 or later and works on every iPhone. It is iPhone only.

A level seems impossible

Every level is solvable. Try folding earlier or later, check which world a crate, lever or cracked floor belongs to, or use a hint. If you still think something is broken, email us with the level number.

Privacy and data
